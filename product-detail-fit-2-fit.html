About this product

Weight gain is one of the most common problems today and can lead to lifestyle conditions such as diabetes, heart problems and hypertension. Fit 2 Fit is an Ayurvedic supplement made from jowar, oats, cumin, flaxseed, kachri and warming spices to support healthy fat metabolism and weight management.

Benefits

  • Helps you stay fit and maintain a healthy body weight
  • Supports reduction of belly fat
  • Helps relieve stress and fatigue and eases gastrointestinal problems
  • Supports digestion and improves metabolism
  • Helpful for hormonal imbalance and PCOD/PCOS related concerns
  • Helps regulate irregular periods
  • Boosts immunity and overall health
  • Helps prevent further fat accumulation

How to use

  • Boil 1 sachet in 200 ml of water until it reduces to 100 ml.
  • Take on an empty stomach, once in the morning and once in the evening.

While using this product, avoid cold drinks, deep-fried and fatty food, pizza, burgers, rice, sugar, potato, banana, maida, ice cream, chocolates, and bakery or junk food.
