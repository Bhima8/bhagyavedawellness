About this product

Poor diet and too little water can lead to kidney stones, which usually form from excess calcium oxalate or other mineral crystals. Symptoms can include fever, vomiting, nausea, severe lower abdomen or back pain, burning during urination or blood in urine. The Kidney Stone Kit pairs Dezotone capsules with Vedatri Churna, made from Pashanbhed, Gokhru, Punarnava and other traditional herbs.

Benefits

  • Traditionally used to help break down and pass small kidney and bladder stones
  • Helps reduce burning sensation in the urinary tract
  • Helps ease stone-related discomfort
  • Herbal Ayurvedic formulation

Ingredients

  • Pashanbhed
  • Gokhru
  • Punarnava
  • Varunsaal
  • Jeshthamadh
  • Apamarg
  • Kulthi

How to use

  • Capsules: take 2 capsules in the morning and 2 capsules in the evening before meals.
  • Churna: boil 1 teaspoon of powder in 200 ml water until it reduces to 100 ml. Take after meals, morning and evening.

Drink plenty of water. If pain, fever or blood in urine continues, see a doctor without delay.
