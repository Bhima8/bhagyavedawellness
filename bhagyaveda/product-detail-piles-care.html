About this product

Piles affect both men and women and are mainly linked to irregular eating habits and constipation. Panchamsam Churna is an Ayurvedic formulation of traditional herbs that strengthens digestion, eases constipation, purifies blood and helps reduce swelling and pain. It is paired with Pitone capsules in this kit.

Benefits

  • Helps reduce pain and swelling
  • Helps relieve itching and burning sensation
  • Supports healing of rectal wounds and reduces bleeding

How to use

  • Capsules: take 2 capsules in the morning and 2 capsules in the evening before meals.
  • Churna: take 1 teaspoon of powder with lukewarm or normal water after meals, morning and evening.

If bleeding is heavy or persistent, consult a doctor.
