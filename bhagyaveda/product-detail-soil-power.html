About this product

In modern farming, keeping the soil fertile is key to quality and yield. Soil Power is an agricultural supplement formulated with Humic Acid, Fulvic Acid and Potassium to improve soil health and support strong, healthy crop growth.

Benefits

  • Improves soil fertility and makes soil looser, softer and more productive
  • Humic and Fulvic Acid make soil nutrients easier for plants to absorb
  • Strengthens root development and nutrient uptake
  • Keeps leaves greener, healthier and more vibrant
  • Supports flowering and fruit setting for better yield
  • Improves size, colour, shine and quality of crops and fruit
  • Helps crops tolerate heat, drought and changing weather
  • Suitable for vegetables, fruit crops, cereals, sugarcane and many other crops

Ingredients

  • Humic Acid
  • Fulvic Acid
  • Potassium (K₂O)

How to use

  • Foliar spray: mix 1 to 2 g of Soil Power per litre of water.
  • Drip irrigation or soil application: use 1 to 2 kg per acre mixed with water.
  • Timing: apply every 15 to 20 days during the crop growth stage for better results.
