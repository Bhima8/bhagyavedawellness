About this product

Modern diets high in oil and fat, adulterated food and sedentary living put a strain on the heart. Well Heart is a food-based supplement made with Arjuna, rock sugar, flaxseeds, bay leaf, black cumin and cinnamon to support natural heart health.

Benefits

  • Supports heart health and cardiovascular function
  • Helps prevent cholesterol build-up in blood vessels
  • Supports arterial health and healthy circulation
  • Helps keep blood pressure in a healthy range
  • Supports immunity and resistance to disease

Ingredients

  • Arjuna
  • Rock Sugar
  • Flaxseeds
  • Bay Leaf
  • Black Cumin
  • Cinnamon

How to use

  • Take 1 sachet in the morning and 1 sachet in the evening on an empty stomach with lukewarm water.

It is recommended to do a Lipid Profile Test before starting Well Heart, and to repeat it after 1 month to track improvement.

Do not stop any prescribed heart medicine without your doctor's advice.
