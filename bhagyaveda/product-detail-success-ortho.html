About this product

Success Ortho is an Ayurvedic herbal formulation made with methi, green tea, nutmeg, amla, hirda, behda, hadjod, chitrak and nishottar. It is traditionally used to support bone strength, joint comfort and a healthy balance of Vata.

Benefits

  • Supports strong bones and joints and helps raise calcium levels in the body
  • Helps relieve joint stiffness and swelling
  • Balances Vata dosha and related disorders
  • Helpful for numbness and tingling in the limbs
  • Supports comfort in the neck, back, waist, shoulder, ankle and knee
  • Traditionally used for spondylitis, sciatica, migraine, rheumatoid arthritis and tennis elbow
  • Helps ease cracking joint sounds and improves joint flexibility
  • Provides natural antioxidants
  • Helps with constipation and digestive discomfort
  • Supports blood circulation, nervous system function and sleep

How to use

  • Take 1 sachet with lukewarm water before breakfast and before dinner.

While using this product, avoid curd, buttermilk, banana, brinjal, sour, spicy and oily food, and cold or refrigerated items.
