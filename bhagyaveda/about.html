Our story

Bhagyaveda joins two Sanskrit-rooted words: Bhagya, meaning good fortune, and Veda, meaning knowledge. Our name carries a simple belief. Good health is the foundation of a fortunate life, and time-tested knowledge is the best place to start looking after it.

We are based in Solapur, Maharashtra, and offer herbal and wellness products inspired by Ayurveda. Our range covers bone and joint care, heart and sugar care, women's wellness, vitality, juices and tonics, digestion and detox, and soil care for farmers.

Every enquiry gets a personal reply. We would rather explain a product clearly and help you choose well than sell you something you do not need.
