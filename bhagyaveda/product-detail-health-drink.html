About this product

Stevia is a natural sweetener extracted from the Stevia plant and is widely used as a sugar substitute. Research points to antioxidant, antimicrobial and antifungal properties. This Health Drink offers stevia as a simple daily wellness supplement and a sugar-free option for people watching their sugar intake.

Benefits

  • Helps support healthy blood sugar levels
  • Helps the body absorb essential nutrients and minerals
  • Supports energy levels
  • Supports healthy weight management
  • Supports physical and mental strength
  • Strengthens immunity and resistance

Ingredients

  • Stevia

How to use

  • Add 10 drops to 250 ml water and take twice daily before meals.
