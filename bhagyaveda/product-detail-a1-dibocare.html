About this product

Diabetes is one of the most common health problems today. It occurs when the body cannot make or use enough insulin, so blood sugar stays high. High blood sugar slows circulation and affects immunity, wound healing, energy and organs like the eyes and kidneys. A1 Dibocare is a herbal formulation to support blood sugar management alongside a healthy lifestyle.

Benefits

  • Helps support healthy blood sugar levels
  • Supports glucose metabolism and pancreatic function
  • Supports the body's natural insulin production
  • Helps reduce triglyceride levels
  • Eases common symptoms such as frequent urination, weakness, fatigue and dry throat
  • Supports the heart, kidneys, liver and nerves

Ingredients

  • Gurmar
  • Vijaysar
  • Methi (Fenugreek)
  • Karela (Bitter Gourd)
  • Jamun
  • Kalmegh
  • Kutki
  • Bel
  • Ashwagandha

How to use

  • If blood sugar is below 200: take 1 capsule each morning and evening before meals.
  • If blood sugar is above 200: take 2 capsules each morning and evening before meals.

Do not stop or change prescribed diabetes medicines without your doctor's advice. Monitor your blood sugar regularly.
